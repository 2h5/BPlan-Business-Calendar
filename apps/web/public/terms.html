1. The service

BPlan is a calendar and task-planning service. The current product includes internal calendar and task tools, optional Google and Microsoft calendar connections, and an AI-assisted Find Time workflow where enabled.

2. Accounts and age

You must be at least 18 years old to use the service. Keep your account information accurate and protect your account credentials.

3. Pro plans

The current Pro feature is Find Time with AI. The service first calculates available time deterministically; an AI provider may rank and explain the resulting candidates. AI suggestions are assistance, not a guarantee that a calendar remains unchanged or conflict-free after the suggestion is made.

Launch pricing is $4.99 per month or $49.99 per year, in USD. There is no free trial or introductory discount currently planned. Plans renew automatically until cancelled, subject to the final checkout disclosure.

4. Cancellation and refunds

Users can cancel through the available billing-management flow. A cancellation stops future renewals, and access continues through the current paid billing period. Charges are generally non-refundable except where required by law or where BPlan chooses to provide a refund.

5. User data

You retain ownership of your calendar, task, and other user-created data. You grant BPlan only the rights needed to process that data to provide the service. The separate Privacy Policy draft describes the current data flows.

6. Acceptable use and termination

Do not use BPlan for abuse, fraud, attacks, unlawful activity, or activity that compromises another person’s data or service. BPlan may suspend or terminate access for abuse, fraud, security issues, or Terms violations.

7. Account deletion

Users may request deletion through the account controls. The current implementation revokes connected calendar grants before deleting the authenticated account. Exact backup and third-party retention details remain a launch decision and must be added before this document is final.

8. Governing-law working assumption

The current working assumption is New Jersey, United States, with initial sales limited to the United States. The seller identity, final governing law, and dispute terms remain subject to founder and legal review.
